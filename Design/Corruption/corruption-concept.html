CORRUPTION v2

Concept prototype for the refactor (Sonnet draft, rough, for Opus to polish). Corrupting no longer uses one of the offered cards: with a charge, CORRUPT opens a second screen listing everything you own. Click CORRUPT in the phone to try it.

1. Try the flow

LV1
CHOOSE AN UPGRADE
CORRUPT
PICK AN UPGRADE TO CORRUPT
DRAG TO SCROLL. EXQUISITE AND CURSED CARDS CANNOT BE CORRUPTED.

1. On the card screen, CORRUPT lights up as soon as a charge is held, with no card selected (it no longer eats a choice). 2. The card screen slides up and the inventory slides in. 3. Tap a card, read the odds and the cost, tap CORRUPT. 4. Everything else shatters, the chosen card glitches, reveal, CONFIRM. 5. Back to the same card screen with the same offer, rerolls and picks untouched. Drag the list to scroll (mouse wheel works too).

Outcome to preview:

Corruption charges:

Sequence (about 3.5 s, reuse the shipped one): red flash, every other card shatters into shards (those fly and get sucked into the chosen card), the card glides to the center, virus glitch (RGB split, slice tears, scanlines, noise, shake), CRT cut to black, reveal as Exquisite (red burst) or Cursed (dull thud), cost typed under the card, one CONFIRM.

2. What changes from the shipped system

  • Entry: the CORRUPT button is enabled by a charge alone. No card needs to be selected, and the three offered cards are not touched. Using it does not consume the level-up choice, rerolls or the offer.
  • Corruption screen: the card screen slides up and out; a second screen slides in from the bottom, a full inventory of every upgrade owned, as cards like the upgrade screen but 3 per row. If it overflows, the list scrolls vertically by dragging (same gesture behaviour as the power-up list on the pause screen: drag, momentum, tap vs drag threshold).
  • Eligible cards: any owned normal-pool card (capped or unlimited). Exquisite and Cursed cards are shown but locked and cannot be selected. A BACK button returns to the card screen with the charge unspent.
  • Cost: the selected card loses one level (LV 3 > 2). The cap is no longer lowered for the run. A card at LV 1 is removed (and can be offered again later like any card, its cap is untouched). Needs PowerUpEffect.CanRemove; cards without a Remove are locked too.
  • Outcome: a random Exquisite or Cursed card, odds by the rarity of the corrupted card (30 / 40 / 55 / 70 / 80% Exquisite, unchanged). The outcome is added to the run (it is a free pick, the player still picks one of the offered cards afterwards).
  • No dowry: a Cursed outcome no longer pays anything (no extra reroll, no guaranteed Rare+ / Legendary card).
  • Confirm is the only choice after the reveal; it slides back to the upgrade screen.
  • Background: the corruption screen has its own flickery look, close to the virus of the sequence: dark blood-red field that drops in brightness in steps, rolling band, scanlines, pixel noise, occasional horizontal tears, jittering RGB-split title. It is on from the moment the screen slides in, and the sequence's virus ramps on top of it.
