CORRUPTION

Concept prototype (Sonnet, rough). Same card screen as the game; CORRUPT sits under Reroll / Confirm and is always optional. Opus will do the final pass on shader, audio and polish.

1. Try the flow

LV1
CHOOSE AN UPGRADE

Select a card. CONFIRM takes it normally. CORRUPT (below) gambles it instead, and only lights up for cards with a capped stack (Executioner, Twin Fission, Laser Focus...). Unlimited cards (Brake Pads, Lucky Orbs, Meteor Ball) cannot be corrupted. Reroll swaps the offer.

Outcome to preview:

Corruption charges:

Sequence (about 3.5 s): 0.0 the other cards shatter into shards, red flash and the dim turns blood-red. 0.4 the chosen card slides to the center, a bit bigger. 0.9 to 2.8 virus glitch: RGB split, slice tears, scanlines, noise, shake, flickering text. 2.8 hard cut to black. 2.95 reveal: red burst (Exquisite) or a dull thud (Cursed), the card shows its new max level. Then one CONFIRM tap, no reroll or swap.

3. Placeholder pools: Exquisite (top row) and Cursed (bottom row)

The real pools will be decided later. A pool card can be unlimited (LV 1) or capped (LV 1/2, 1/3). The outcome card is picked at random from its pool and is unrelated to the card that was corrupted.

4. Rules (from the user)

  • Always optional. With a charge the player still chooses: CONFIRM (normal pick) or CORRUPT. The card screen keeps its current design; CORRUPT is a third button under Reroll and Confirm, shown only while a charge is held.
  • Charge source: new Epic card GLITCH CORE ("+1 corruption charge", unlimited picks, each gives a charge). Name is a proposal.
  • Only capped cards. A card can be corrupted only if it has a max stack of 2 or more. Unlimited cards and 1-stack cards (Shield Plus, Meltdown...) cannot.
  • Cost: corrupting a card lowers that card's own max stack by one for the rest of the run (Twin Fission LV 1/2 becomes 1/1). Same cost for either outcome.
  • Outcome: Exquisite (red, above Legendary) or Cursed (dark grey, a real downside). Odds proposal: 55% / 45%. The result is a random card from the Exquisite pool or the Cursed pool (pools decided later), not a variant of the corrupted card. Pool cards can be unlimited or capped. The result replaces the pick (the player does not also get the corrupted card).
  • Pause screen: Exquisite and Cursed cards keep their own entry and color. Greed becomes Exquisite.
