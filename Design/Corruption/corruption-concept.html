CORRUPTION

Concept prototype (Sonnet, rough). Exquisite and Malus rarities, the CORRUPT button on the level-up screen, and the "computer virus" transformation. Opus will do the final pass on shader, audio and polish.

1. Try the flow

Pick a card (tap it), then press CORRUPT. The other cards shatter, the chosen one drifts to the center and glitches, then turns Exquisite or Malus. Confirm with one tap. No reroll or swap.

Timeline (about 3.5 s): 0.0 other cards shatter into pixel shards, hot red flash, low alarm. 0.4 chosen card slides to the center and grows. 0.4 to 2.4 glitch: RGB split, slice tears, scanlines, noise, shake, flickering text. 2.4 hard cut to black for 0.1 s. 2.5 reveal: red burst (Exquisite) or a dull thud and dust (Malus). 3.0 CONFIRM appears.

3. Example pairs

Each corruptible card has an Exquisite (a bigger version, often with a twist) and a Malus (a real downside, possibly with a small upside). Text is placeholder for design only.

4. Rules to settle (proposal)

  • Charges: one Epic card gives corruption charges (1 per pick). The CORRUPT button shows with at least 1 charge and costs 1.
  • Corruptible: any card with stacks. Not the one-shots (Meltdown, Phoenix Ball, Bonus Magnet, Wide Choice) or the Epic corruption card itself.
  • Outcome odds (to tune): 55% Exquisite, 45% Malus. Another card could shift the odds.
  • A corrupted card is a separate entry in the pause screen list with its own color: red bar, glitching name for Exquisite; grey bar, dim text for Malus.
  • Greed becomes Exquisite. Candidates to move up: Paddle Cannon, Satellite, Meltdown, Phoenix Ball.
  • Malus must be playable, not a pure trap: a trade (e.g. big power for a cost) so a gambler can still win.
